# Hidden Assumptions in Fundamental Physics: A Dialectical Audit
2026-08-11

---

This report treats foundational assumptions in physics as fallible working hypotheses rather than settled facts or errors. Each is evaluated for rationale, tensions, alternatives, discriminating tests, and whether departures reduce or merely relocate theoretical complexity. Confidence levels are stated explicitly; consensus is not manufactured where none exists.

## 1. Invariance and Constancy of the Speed of Light

**Thesis (orthodox):** Local Lorentz invariance (LLI) — the postulate that the speed of light $$c$$ is a fixed, frame-independent invariant locally — underlies special and general relativity and, by extension, essentially all of modern physics. Empirical support is extraordinarily broad: clock-comparison experiments bound anisotropy/boost violations in the Standard-Model Extension (SME) framework at levels of $$10^{-27}$$ to $$10^{-32}$$ GeV for various coefficients[^1]. Astrophysical tests using gamma-ray bursts, blazars, and pair-production thresholds push Lorentz-violation energy scales into the range of several times the Planck energy for some operators, with no confirmed violation detected to date[^2].

**Antithesis (heterodox):** Varying-speed-of-light (VSL) cosmologies were proposed partly to solve the horizon, flatness, and monopole problems without inflation, and partly motivated by anomalous quasar spectroscopy suggesting redshift-dependence in the fine-structure constant $$\alpha = e^2/(\hbar c)$$[^3]. The evidence remains "slim," as characterized even by VSL proponents, and constraints from Oklo (natural nuclear reactor), quasar absorption spectra, and atomic clocks are tight but not fully decisive, with reported $$\Delta\alpha/\alpha$$ dipole signals at the $$10^{-6}$$ to $$10^{-5}$$ level in some quasar samples[^4]. Current bounds on the present-day rate of change derived from spacecraft ranging and helioseismological constraints on $$\dot G/G$$ give $$|\dot c/c| \le 0.55\times10^{-12}\,\text{yr}^{-1}$$[^5][^6]. Supernova-based tests of "minimally extended VSL" models find the data statistically compatible with either constant or mildly varying $$c$$, generally favoring the constant-$$c$$ null hypothesis at high significance[^7], though other analyses using Pantheon+ under dark-energy parameterizations report 1σ nonzero drift ranges depending strongly on the assumed dark-energy equation of state[^8][^9].

**Discriminating tests:** Strong gravitational lensing combined with Type Ia supernovae to test model-independent invariance of $$c$$ at different redshifts; Square Kilometre Array (SKA) forecasts of ~1% (3σ) or 0.1% (1σ with 10x error reduction) sensitivity to variation in $$c$$[^4]; continued clock-comparison and astrophysical LIV bounds via Cherenkov Telescope Array (CTA) and LHAASO[^2].

**Assessment:** VSL/LIV departures are a legitimate minority research program with severe, tightening constraints. They do not currently reduce complexity relative to ΛCDM plus inflation — they typically add free functions ($$c(t)$$, $$n$$) without independent motivation, and current data "favor a constant speed of light" [^7] at high confidence. Confidence: high that LLI holds to the constraints listed; low-to-moderate that any of the specific anomalies (α-dipole, cosmic ray thresholds) require new physics rather than systematics.

## 2. Spacetime Ontology: Continuum vs. Discreteness, Background (In)Dependence

Three major discretization programs — Loop Quantum Gravity (LQG), Causal Set Theory (CST), and String Theory — differ sharply in their foundational commitments. LQG derives discreteness from SU(2) gauge quantization of geometric operators (area, volume), predicting quantized spectra and resolving classical singularities, but struggles to recover smooth macroscopic spacetime in the continuum limit[^10][^11]. CST postulates discreteness axiomatically via partially ordered sets and reconstructs approximate Lorentz invariance and causal structure from combinatorics[^11][^12]. String theory derives discreteness from the string scale and Regge slope, is a "top-down" unification aiming to include gravity and matter together, and yields a semiclassical derivation of black-hole entropy, but the resulting landscape of ~$$10^{500}$$ vacua undermines predictivity[^10][^13].

Quantum-cellular-automaton (QCA) models bridge discrete quantum-information processing and continuum QED, predicting a deviation from $$c$$ and small spatial anisotropies; current astrophysical/experimental LIV bounds already constrain the implied lattice spacing near or below the Planck scale, showing that "Lorentz invariance appears robust experimentally" even if it is emergent from an underlying discrete structure[^14]. This is a genuine synthesis candidate: discreteness at the Planck scale is not excluded, but any discrete substrate must reproduce LLI to exquisite precision at accessible energies — a strong constraint on model space rather than a refutation of discreteness per se.

No experimental test currently discriminates decisively among LQG, CST, string theory, or emergent-spacetime/holographic approaches; all remain empirically underdetermined at accessible energies[^11]. This underdetermination should not be mistaken for evidence favoring any one program — a common conflation this report explicitly guards against.

## 3. Quantum Foundations: Measurement, Locality, and Statistical Independence

**Orthodox baseline:** Standard quantum mechanics (Copenhagen-style or decoherence-based) assumes unitarity, the Born rule, and — crucially for Bell-inequality arguments — Statistical Independence (measurement settings uncorrelated with hidden variables) and no-signaling locality. Bell test violations are robust and loophole-closed across many experiments, generally interpreted as ruling out local hidden-variable theories that respect Statistical Independence[^15].

**Heterodox challengers — superdeterminism and retrocausality:** Superdeterminism denies Statistical Independence, proposing that measurement settings and hidden variables share correlated causal ancestry, potentially rooted in the initial conditions of the universe[^16][^17]. Proponents (Hossenfelder, Palmer) argue this could solve the measurement problem and explain apparent nonlocality without invoking either indefinite reality or action-at-a-distance[^18][^19]. Critics counter that any deterministic underpinning conflicts with the noncommutativity underlying quantum indeterminacy, and that superdeterminism risks undermining the very possibility of experimental inference (the "conspiracy" objection)[^20]. A formal taxonomy paper by Adlam, Hance, Hossenfelder, and Palmer (2023–2024) attempts to disambiguate superdeterminism, retrocausality, and future-input-dependence, noting persistent terminological confusion in the field[^15][^21]. Retrocausal models (transactional interpretation, two-time formalism, retrocausal Bohmian mechanics) offer an alternative resolution that preserves Statistical Independence while allowing correlations to propagate backward in time within the light cone structure[^22][^23].

**Discriminating tests:** Proposed near-model-independent tests of superdeterminism exist in principle (Hossenfelder & Palmer outline testable signatures)[^16], though none has yet produced a positive result; this remains a minority research program actively debated at dedicated workshops (e.g., the Bonn/Frankfurt Superdeterminism and Retrocausality workshops)[^24].

**Assessment:** Superdeterminism and retrocausality are legitimate minority hypotheses addressing a real conceptual gap (the measurement problem), not pseudoscience, but they currently lack any confirmed empirical signature distinguishing them from standard QM. They add explanatory complexity (a specific correlation structure reaching back to the Big Bang) without yet demonstrating a compensating reduction elsewhere. Confidence: the measurement problem is a genuine open problem (high confidence); superdeterminism/retrocausality as its resolution is speculative (low-moderate confidence), actively studied by credentialed researchers, and should not be dismissed by novelty bias alone nor accepted by contrarian appeal alone.

## 4. Cosmological Principle: Homogeneity, Isotropy, and the Cosmic Dipole Anomaly

**Rationale for the Cosmological Principle (CP):** The assumption that the universe is homogeneous and isotropic on large scales underlies the FLRW metric and the entire ΛCDM framework; it has substantial indirect support from CMB isotropy at the 1-part-in-100,000 level.

**High-value anomaly — the cosmic dipole tension:** Multiple independent surveys of radio galaxies and quasars (NVSS, RACS-low, CatWISE, LoTSS-DR2) report a dipole in the large-scale matter distribution that is 2–4 times larger than expected from pure kinematic (Doppler/aberration) boosting inferred from the CMB dipole, aligned in direction with the CMB dipole but anomalously large in amplitude[^25][^26]. A 2025 analysis (Böhme et al., reported in a 2026 review) finds a radio dipole excess of $$d_{\rm obs}/d_{\rm exp} = 3.67 \pm 0.49$$, corresponding to 5.4σ significance[^27]. A Bayesian joint analysis finds Planck in severe (>5σ) tension with CatWISE, strong tension with RACS, and moderate tension with NVSS, while noting the RACS discrepancy from the other radio catalogs may indicate a systematic issue specific to that survey[^28]. Mundane explanations proposed first, per post-normal science practice, include: survey selection effects, calibration systematics in flux-limited radio catalogs, and evolving source populations; these have been examined and, per Böhme et al. and Secrest et al., not fully account for the excess[^25][^27]. If confirmed as physical rather than systematic, this would represent either an anisotropic universe on scales >1 Gpc or a breakdown in the standard kinematic interpretation of the CMB dipole itself — a serious foundational problem for ΛCDM[^25][^26].

**Discriminating test:** The tension is not yet definitive; an estimated $$\mathcal{O}(10^6)$$ radio sources are needed to reach unambiguous 5σ significance across catalogs, achievable with upcoming SKA surveys[^28]. This is one of the report's top decisive near-term tests (see Section 9).

**Assessment:** This is a robust, statistically significant (multi-survey, >5σ in the strongest dataset), but not yet fully cross-validated anomaly. Confidence: moderate-high that a real tension exists between datasets; low-moderate that it demands abandoning the CP rather than reflecting an unmodeled systematic in specific catalogs (e.g., RACS).

## 5. Dark Matter, Dark Energy, and Cosmological Tensions

**Dark matter — orthodox (particle) hypothesis:** Cold dark matter (CDM) is strongly motivated by CMB acoustic peaks, large-scale structure formation, and gravitational lensing, none of which are naturally explained by baryonic matter alone under GR[^29][^30]. Direct-detection experiments (WIMPs, axions) have returned only null results after decades of increasingly sensitive searches[^31][^32].

**Heterodox alternative — Modified Newtonian Dynamics (MOND)/modified gravity:** MOND explains galactic rotation curves and several scaling relations (e.g., baryonic Tully-Fisher relation) without dark matter, using a single acceleration scale $$a_0$$[^29]. Recent tests are mixed and actively contested: a Chandrasekhar dynamical-friction analysis of the Magellanic Clouds' orbital stability is claimed by Kroupa and Hernandez (2025) to reject particle dark matter at >5σ on galactic scales[^31]; conversely, a 2026 kinetic Sunyaev-Zel'dovich analysis on 30–230 Mpc scales finds data strongly favoring dark matter and disfavoring MOND's characteristic force-law transition at 3.3σ, with forecasts of 10σ discriminating power from future surveys[^33]. SPARC galaxy rotation curve fits are also reported to statistically prefer dark matter over MOND in a plurality of cases[^34]. Aether-scalar-tensor (relativistic MOND) theory predicts exotic compact objects ("black hole mimickers" and "Shapiro-free lenses") with unusual gravitational-wave echoes and lensing signatures that are in principle observationally distinguishable from GR+CDM predictions[^35].

**Cosmological parameter tensions:**
- **Hubble tension:** A 2026 community-consensus recalibration achieved 1% precision on the locally measured $$H_0$$, and rather than resolving the tension, sharpened it to >5σ relative to early-universe (CMB-based) predictions[^36].
- **S8/σ8 tension:** Weak-lensing surveys have historically found lower matter-clustering amplitudes than CMB-based ΛCDM predictions, at levels of 1.5–3σ depending on the survey; but this tension shows signs of resolving with newer, more precise datasets — KiDS Legacy (2025) is consistent with Planck at <1σ, while DES Year 6 (2026) still shows 2.4–2.7σ tension, indicating the anomaly may be partly systematic (survey-dependent) rather than universal new physics[^37][^38][^39].

**Assessment:** Dark matter as particle matter remains the mainstream, better cross-validated position overall (structure formation, CMB, cluster lensing, and now SZ effect at large scales), but MOND-type alternatives retain some galactic-scale successes not yet fully explained by dark matter models and represent a legitimate, falsifiable minority program. Confidence: moderate-high in particle dark matter's overall explanatory dominance; the specific claim of "particle dark matter ruled out at 5σ" via dynamical friction is disputed and not broadly accepted by the mainstream community, illustrating a case where strong claims should be flagged for independent verification rather than taken at face value.

## 6. Exotic Compact Objects vs. Classical Black Holes

**Rationale for horizonless alternatives:** Gravastars, boson stars, fuzzballs, and other exotic compact objects (ECOs) are motivated by attempts to resolve the black-hole information paradox and singularity problem without invoking an event horizon, often inspired by quantum-gravity considerations (e.g., firewall/fuzzball proposals)[^40][^41].

**Observational discriminants:**
- **Gravitational-wave echoes:** ECOs with reflective or partially reflective surfaces are predicted to produce late-time "echo" signals following the main ringdown, distinct from a pure black-hole quasinormal-mode spectrum[^42][^43]. Current LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA searches (GWTC-3 catalog) find no statistically significant evidence of echoes, setting upper limits on echo amplitude[^44][^45]. A tentative ~4.2σ echo claim in GW170817 has not been confirmed by subsequent independent reanalyses, which find no statistical evidence in O1/O2 data[^46].
- **Population constraints:** Spin-distribution analysis of 104 LVK compact-binary events constrains ultracompact totally-reflective ECOs (with surface displacement $$\epsilon<10^{-30}$$ from the horizon) to compose no more than 25–28% of the observed compact-object population at 90% credibility; the Einstein Telescope could tighten this to <20% within a single day of data[^47][^48].
- **Physical instability arguments:** ECOs with Planck-scale-close surfaces are shown to be gravitationally unstable under ordinary accretion and gravitational-wave absorption, forcing collapse into a black hole via the Hoop conjecture unless the surface sits a macroscopic distance from the would-be horizon or violates the null energy condition — a serious theoretical tension for the most "interesting" (near-horizon, quantum-gravity-motivated) ECO models[^40].
- **Tidal Love numbers:** Black holes have exactly zero tidal Love numbers in GR; any confirmed nonzero measurement in a binary inspiral would constitute smoking-gun evidence for a horizonless object, though distinguishing it from an ordinary neutron star remains difficult[^40].

**Assessment:** ECOs are a serious, mathematically well-developed, and actively falsifiable research program, not fringe speculation, but current observational data (echo non-detection, stability arguments) disfavor the most exotic near-Planckian variants while leaving room for less compact "fuzzy" horizon alternatives. Confidence: high that classical GR black holes remain the best-supported description at current sensitivity; moderate that upcoming third-generation detectors (Einstein Telescope, Cosmic Explorer) could meaningfully test remaining ECO parameter space within the next decade.

## 7. Mathematical Frameworks: Explanatory Gain vs. Reformulation

Geometric algebra (Clifford algebra) offers substantial **computational and conceptual** advantages — coordinate-free treatment of rotations, unification of complex numbers/quaternions/spinors, and compact reformulation of Maxwell's equations and the Dirac equation — but the mainstream literature treats it primarily as a reformulation of existing physics rather than a source of new empirical predictions[^49][^50][^51]. Extended/Clifford-space relativity (polyvector coordinates) is a more ambitious heterodox program aiming to unify GR and the Standard Model, with proposed connections to emergent dark energy and maximal acceleration, but remains speculative and largely untested[^52][^53].

Holographic/emergent-gravity approaches (AdS/CFT-inspired, entanglement-based) have produced some of the most productive recent theoretical progress, deriving spacetime geometry from entanglement entropy of many-body quantum systems, and proposing testable signatures such as gravitational decoherence noise spectra detectable via atom interferometry[^54][^55]. This is a case where mathematical reformulation (holography) plausibly reduces complexity by deriving gravity from more fundamental quantum-informational postulates, rather than merely adding new entities — a genuine synthesis candidate.

The string theory landscape/swampland debate exemplifies the core methodological tension this report is designed to probe: the landscape's ~$$10^{500}$$ vacua threaten falsifiability, and critics (Woit, Smolin) argue the theory "fits any data" and thus tests nothing[^56][^57]; defenders argue swampland conjectures (e.g., no global symmetries, weak gravity conjecture) impose genuinely falsifiable constraints, and that current de Sitter cosmological observations are in tension with conventional swampland criteria — a testable, currently unresolved conflict[^58][^59][^60]. Philosophical analysis argues the landscape problem itself may rest on "question-begging" assumptions and is not, properly understood, evidence against falsifiability[^61].

**Assessment:** Geometric algebra: high confidence it offers genuine computational/pedagogical value; low confidence (and little claim in the literature) that it yields new falsifiable physics beyond reformulation. String landscape/swampland: unresolved, actively contested even within the string community; confidence in eventual falsifiability is low-moderate given the vastness of the solution space.

## 8. Metascientific and Post-Normal Considerations

Several structural features of the field create systematic risk of premature theory entrenchment or premature abandonment:
- **Publication and citation dynamics** favor incremental extensions of dominant paradigms (ΛCDM, string theory, WIMP dark matter) over minority programs, partly because null results and negative searches are harder to publish prominently, even though they carry real information (e.g., accumulating WIMP exclusion limits)[^30][^32].
- **Model-selection and benchmark dependence**: cosmological tension significances (Hubble, S8) are sensitive to which datasets and pipelines are combined, and improved calibration has in some cases sharpened rather than resolved tensions (Hubble) while apparently reducing others (S8, per KiDS Legacy), showing the field is capable of self-correction but also that headline "tension" numbers are analysis-dependent and should be reported with method-level caveats[^36][^38].
- **Novelty bias vs. institutional conservatism**: the swampland/landscape debate and the superdeterminism debate both illustrate the field's genuine, unresolved disagreement about what counts as a legitimate minority hypothesis vs. unfalsifiable speculation; dedicated workshops (e.g., Bonn/Frankfurt on superdeterminism, FQXI-funded work on swampland alternatives) indicate credentialed institutional space exists for heterodox programs, which argues against blanket dismissal but does not itself validate the programs[^24][^59].
- **Post-normal science framing** is directly applicable to cosmological-tension interpretation: stakes are high (foundations of ΛCDM), uncertainties are large and not fully quantifiable via standard statistics alone (systematic vs. physical origin of tensions is often undetermined), and broadened quality assessment (multiple independent probes, transparent systematic-error budgets, open data) is appropriate — without lowering the evidentiary bar for claiming new physics[^62].

## 10. High-Value Anomalies (Mundane Explanations Stated First)

1. **Cosmic dipole anomaly** — first check: radio-survey calibration/selection systematics (especially RACS-specific discrepancies); if not systematic, implies breakdown of the kinematic CMB-dipole interpretation or superhorizon anisotropy[^28][^27].
2. **Hubble tension** — first check: local distance-ladder systematics (Cepheid calibration, supernova standardization); a 2026 consensus recalibration reduced measurement uncertainty but sharpened rather than resolved the tension, disfavoring a purely systematic origin[^36].
3. **S8/σ8 tension** — first check: baryonic feedback and intrinsic-alignment modeling in weak-lensing pipelines; recent KiDS Legacy results suggest much of the historical tension may indeed be systematic/pipeline-dependent[^38][^37].
4. **Quasar/fine-structure-constant spatial dipole (Webb et al. and successors)** — first check: instrumental/calibration systematics across telescopes (Keck vs. VLT); dipole signal direction has some consistency across studies but magnitude and significance remain disputed[^4][^3].
5. **Tentative GW echo signals (e.g., 4.2σ claim in GW170817)** — first check: instrumental noise/glitches; not confirmed by independent reanalyses, currently best explained as statistical fluctuation or pipeline artifact[^46].
6. **MOND galactic-scale successes** — first check: baryonic feedback and halo-response modeling within CDM; recent SZ-effect and SPARC analyses lean toward CDM at larger scales, but a fully unified galactic-to-cosmological CDM fit to the same precision as MOND at galactic scales remains an active research gap[^33][^34].

## 11. Ten Decisive Near-Term Tests

1. SKA radio continuum surveys reaching $$\mathcal{O}(10^6)$$ sources to resolve the cosmic dipole tension at unambiguous 5σ significance[^28].
2. Continued/expanded weak-lensing surveys (LSST/Rubin, Euclid) to determine whether the S8 tension is systematic or physical, following the KiDS-Legacy vs. DES-Y6 split[^37][^38].
3. Third-generation gravitational-wave detectors (Einstein Telescope, Cosmic Explorer) placing sub-percent-level constraints on ECO population fraction and echo amplitude[^47].
4. Precision tidal-Love-number measurements in compact-binary inspirals to distinguish black holes from horizonless compact objects[^40].
5. Sunyaev-Zel'dovich and lensing tests of MOND at 100+ Mpc scales, aiming for the forecast 10σ discriminating power against CDM[^33].
6. Continued atomic-clock and astrophysical (CTA, LHAASO) tests tightening Lorentz-invariance-violation bounds toward and beyond the Planck scale[^2][^1].
7. Independent local-universe $$H_0$$ measurements via new standard-candle/siren methods (gravitational-wave standard sirens) to test whether the 2026 Hubble-tension sharpening persists under an independent method[^36].
8. High-redshift quasar/absorption-line surveys (extending Webb et al.-style multiplet techniques) with cross-telescope calibration to resolve the disputed α-variation dipole[^4][^3].
9. Tabletop atom-interferometry tests for gravitationally-induced decoherence/entanglement, discriminating coherent quantum gravity from emergent/entropic gravity models[^55].
10. Continued direct-detection dark-matter experiments (next-generation WIMP/axion searches) — a persistent null result across the remaining viable parameter space would meaningfully shift Bayesian credibility toward modified-gravity alternatives[^30][^32].

## 12. Final Research Agenda

**Established problems (broad consensus, active refinement):** Hubble tension, S8 tension (partially resolving), dark-matter direct-detection non-observation, quantum measurement problem, non-unification of GR and quantum field theory, black-hole information paradox.

**Promising speculative programs (legitimate minority hypotheses, testable):** Exotic compact objects and GW echo searches; MOND/modified-gravity as galactic-scale phenomenology requiring reconciliation with cosmological-scale CDM successes; holographic/emergent gravity and entanglement-based spacetime; causal-set and loop-quantum-gravity discreteness programs constrained by but not excluded by LLI bounds; superdeterminism/retrocausality as a structurally coherent (if empirically untested) resolution of the measurement problem; dynamical-tension string models as a response to swampland/de Sitter tension.

**Claims currently unsupported by credible evidence:** Variable speed of light at levels detectable by current instruments (data favor constant c at high significance); "particle dark matter falsified at 5σ" via dynamical friction (disputed, non-consensus claim); confirmed gravitational-wave echoes (no independently replicated detection); confirmed superdeterministic or retrocausal empirical signatures (no positive test yet exists); string-theory landscape as an empirically predictive framework (acknowledged even by proponents to lack direct tests at accessible energies).

Across all domains, the dialectical pattern is consistent: mainstream frameworks retain the strongest cumulative empirical support and internal coherence, but each carries genuine, well-documented tensions (fine-tuning, unexplained parameters, dataset-dependent significance) that justify continued, well-resourced investigation of alternatives — provided those alternatives are held to the same falsifiability and parsimony standards, and provided anomalies are first checked against systematic and mundane explanations before being promoted to evidence of new physics.
